I am a doctoral candidate at the [University of Georgia](https://www.math.uga.edu/) where I am advised by [Akram Alishahi](https://akramalishahi.github.io/). 
We co-organize the _Topology Working Seminar_ at UGA. 
I am interested in questions related to surfaces in 4-manifolds, smooth concordance invariants, and link homology.
